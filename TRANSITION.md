This is a deliberate transition, not a final farewell to the work.

## Preservation of inherited work

Existing source code, language-design work, Universal User-Oriented Programming (UOP) principles, research, technical documentation, examples and other useful contributions are retained as inherited reference material.

Their preservation does not represent an active product launch, an operating PL business, or a commitment to resume development.

The former names **Nervestack**, **NSPL** and **NNI** may remain in historical records, source identifiers, file extensions, repository paths and technical references where changing them would disrupt traceability or functionality. They are not approval of those names as current branding.

The underlying syntax, architecture and functionality are not being redesigned or discarded merely because the former branding is deprecated. Any technical changes require a separate decision.

## Naming and identity safeguards

No replacement name, acronym, logo, tagline, domain, source-file extension or legal/trademark status is approved by this notice. Do not infer or introduce one.

The repository name and internal file/folder names may be reconsidered in a later, deliberate repository-wide identity pass. Until then, preserve Git history, technical continuity and useful inherited material.

## Communication

The transition should be communicated with clarity, dignity and care. Describe it as a new chapter taking shape and an identity under review. Do not present it as a final farewell, a completed rebrand, or an announcement of a successor that has not been selected.
